# Matinha Theme Park Horror Game

Godot 4.7 project. Open the folder in Godot and press F5 to walk around the park.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to run, Space to jump, Esc to free the mouse (click to recapture).

## Map

`scenes/park_map.tscn` is a plain blockout of the first section, laid out from snow's sketch (about 1 px = 0.12 m): the entrance wall with two arches, the parking lot below it, flat areas for the rocking pirate ship, chair swings, Minhocão coaster and bumper cars, and solid blocks for the bathrooms and ticket stand. Everything solid has collision and invisible walls keep you inside the park and the parking lot. No rides are built or scripted yet.
